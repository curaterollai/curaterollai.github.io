CurateRoll AI

Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

In short: CurateRoll AI analyses your photos entirely on your iPhone. Your photos, and everything the app learns from them, never leave your device. We don't collect, store or share any personal data, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking.

Who we are

CurateRoll AI is an iOS app made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). If you have any question about this policy or your data, email curaterollai@gmail.com.

Your photos

CurateRoll AI asks for access to your photo library so it can help you clean it up. You choose how much access to give: your whole library, only selected photos, or none. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

  • On-device analysis. The app uses Apple’s on-device frameworks (Vision and Photos) to look at each photo and its metadata, such as the date, whether it is a screenshot or a favourite, sharpness and the number of people in it. This happens on your iPhone. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • Photos stored in iCloud. If you use iCloud Photos, your iPhone may download a preview of a photo from Apple’s iCloud so it can be analysed. That transfer is between your device and Apple, under Apple’s privacy policy. We have no access to it.
  • What the app keeps. The results of the analysis, your keep/delete swipes and the personalised model trained from them are saved only in the app’s private storage on your iPhone. They are not sent anywhere and are deleted when you delete the app.
  • Deleting photos. The app never deletes a photo without your approval. When you confirm a clean-up, iOS asks you to confirm again, and the deleted photos go to your Recently Deleted album, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a subscription is active. Apple’s handling of your payment is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Feedback emails

If you choose “Give feedback”, the app opens your own email app with a new message to us. The message includes the app version, your iOS version and your device type, to help us fix problems. You can edit or remove any of this before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to improve the app. We keep them for as long as needed for that, and you can ask us to delete them at any time.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t collect or store any personal data on our own servers. The app has no servers of its own.
  • We don’t use analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.
  • We don’t sell or share any data with anyone.

Apple may give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as download numbers, and crash reports if you’ve chosen to share them with developers in your iPhone settings). These don’t identify you.

Children

CurateRoll AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect any information from children.

Your rights

Because your data stays on your device, you are in full control of it. Deleting the app removes everything it has stored. Under data protection laws such as the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, you have the right to access, correct or delete any personal data we hold about you (in practice, only emails you have sent us). To use these rights, email curaterollai@gmail.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will also tell you in the app.
