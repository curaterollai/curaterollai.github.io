Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email curaterollai@gmail.com. We usually reply within two working days. You can also tap … → Give feedback in the app.

Frequently asked questions

How does CurateRoll AI decide what to delete?

You swipe through 50 photos: left to delete, right to keep. The app learns your taste from those swipes and suggests which other photos you’d probably delete. You review every suggestion before anything happens, and each clean-up makes it smarter.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is analysed on your iPhone. See our Privacy Policy.

I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes. Deleted photos go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for up to 30 days.

The analysis seems slow or skips some photos.

The first analysis of a large library can take a while. Keep the app open and your iPhone charging. If you use iCloud Photos with “Optimise iPhone Storage”, some older photos are only in iCloud. The app analyses whatever preview is already on your iPhone and skips photos that can’t be downloaded in time. It tries them again later.

The app only sees some of my photos.

You may have given access to selected photos only. Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → CurateRoll AI and choose Full Access.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CurateRoll AI → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid being charged again. For refunds, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

I already subscribed but the app shows the subscription screen.

Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with, then tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.
